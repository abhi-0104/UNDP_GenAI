Over 35 years, MIGA has issued USD 70 billion in guarantees, with only 11 claims paid, and operates with a 76:1 leverage ratio without having had a capital infusion since 1988. However, most activities focus on upper middle-income countries, with FY22 marking the first time that insurance in LICs exceeded 10% of new issuance volume. (CGDEV)
